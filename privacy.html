Privacy notice

Last updated 21 November 2026

Black Hole Light Paths is a free educational simulation run by Nader Eloshaiker as a personal, non-commercial project. This notice explains what data the site collects and the choices you have.

The simulation

The simulation runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you do with the controls is sent anywhere or stored on a server. The site has no accounts, forms or comments.

Visit counting with GoatCounter

The site counts visits with GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-friendly analytics service. It is used only to see how many people visit and where they come from. It does not use cookies, does not store your IP address, does not track you across websites, and is not used for advertising.

For each page view GoatCounter records:

  • the page you viewed and the website that linked you here, if any
  • your browser and operating system, worked out from your browser's user-agent
  • your screen size and preferred language
  • your country, worked out from your IP address, which is then discarded

To tell a new visit from a repeat page view, GoatCounter uses a short-lived, anonymised hash that cannot be turned back into your IP address and is discarded after a few hours. None of this can identify you, so the site does not ask for consent. See GoatCounter's privacy policy for details.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

Other services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log your IP address for security and to operate the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address to deliver them.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete data about you. The visit counts contain nothing that identifies you, so there is no personal data to access or delete. If you would rather not be counted at all, any common content blocker will stop the GoatCounter script. For any question about this notice, please open an issue on GitHub.
